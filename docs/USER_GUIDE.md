# User Guide

## HOA Admin

Sign in at `/admin`. Review pending accounts, manage homeowner and user records, configure dues, review payments, process complaints and requests, issue/revoke certificates, publish announcements, and use Reports & Exports. Apply date/status filters before downloading; use **Queue CSV** for large datasets, then refresh the page and download the completed private file from **My queued exports** within seven days. System Settings controls the delinquency threshold, notification types, HOA identity, and temporary Staff/Homeowner panel availability. Audit Logs are read-only and exportable.

## HOA Staff

Sign in at `/staff`. Staff can perform operational homeowner, dues-schedule, payment, complaint, request, contact, announcement, certificate, and limited report work. Staff may create, edit, and deactivate dues schedules but cannot delete them. Filtered and queued CSV exports are available for the allowed report set. Staff cannot access users, roles, audit logs, system settings, full payment history, financial dashboard aggregates, permanent deletion, or restore operations.

## Homeowner

Register through the public site, wait for HOA approval, verify the email address, then sign in at `/homeowner`. The dashboard shows outstanding dues and active cases. Use Outstanding Dues to submit a private proof of payment, Payment History for receipts, My Complaints and My Requests for submissions, Certificates for secure downloads, Announcements for current notices, and My Profile for personal/property details and password changes.

Uploads accept only the listed PDF/image/document types and size limits. A record belonging to another homeowner is intentionally returned as not found. Contact the HOA office if an email link expires or a record appears incorrect.
